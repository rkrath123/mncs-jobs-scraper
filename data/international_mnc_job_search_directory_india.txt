International MNC Job-Search Directory in India

Cross-industry company database for IT, infrastructure, cloud, DevOps, SRE, HPC and engineering roles

Prepared: 11 November 2026
Geographic focus: India
Purpose: Build a broad target-employer list across industries, not just
IT services.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Important scope and verification note

There is no practical, authoritative list of every multinational company
in every sector. The directory below is a broad working database of
major international groups that have had a meaningful Indian business,
office, subsidiary, engineering/R&D centre, GCC, manufacturing
footprint, or recurring hiring presence.

A company appearing here is not automatically confirmed to have a
current physical office or open vacancy in India. Some global groups
operate in India through subsidiaries, factories, project sites,
distributors, franchisees, airline routes, or partners. These are
different from an India office or technology centre.

Before applying, verify: 1. The company’s official India website and
current locations page. 2. Whether the relevant Indian legal entity /
office is active. 3. Whether the role is employed in India or is an
overseas role. 4. Whether the team is a GCC, engineering centre,
shared-services centre, plant IT team, field operation, or sales office.
5. The exact work location and hiring entity on the job posting.

Legend - Office/GCC/R&D target: Strong candidate to investigate for an
India office, technology centre, engineering centre or GCC. - India
operating footprint: May have manufacturing, project, commercial,
field-service, subsidiary or partner operations; check for a direct
office and IT hiring. - Global target / verify: International company
worth knowing, but current India office or direct hiring should not be
assumed.

This is a company directory, not an endorsement or ranking. Corporate
structures, brands and ownership change; subsidiaries and brands may be
grouped under their parent.

37. Final verification checklist before applying

For every company, capture:

  Field                What to record
  -------------------- ----------------------------------------------
  Company              Official global company name
  Sector               Industry vertical
  India presence       Office, GCC, R&D, plant, project or sales
  India cities         Actual hiring locations
  Official website     Corporate website
  Careers page         Official job portal
  Target role          DevOps, SRE, Linux, HPC, Cloud, etc.
  Job link             Specific vacancy URL
  Employment entity    Indian subsidiary or employer
  Verification date    Date you checked the information
  Application status   Not applied / Applied / Interview / Rejected

Final note

This is a broad, multi-sector international employer directory designed
to reduce the chance of overlooking relevant industries. It is not
possible to guarantee that it contains every MNC worldwide, and
inclusion does not establish a current Indian office or vacancy. Treat
it as a discovery database and verify each employer’s India presence and
hiring entity before applying.

For your job search, prioritize companies with India-based technology
centres, GCCs, engineering centres, data centres, R&D teams and large
production infrastructure teams. These are generally more relevant than
a company that only sells products or operates a sales office in India.
